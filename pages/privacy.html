Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how CricketHub collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last Updated: May 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CricketHub ("we," "us," or "our"), accessible at crickethub.co.in, collects, uses, and shares information about you when you visit our website. By using our website, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you visit or interact with CricketHub:

Personal Information You Provide:

  • Contact Form Data: When you submit our contact form, we collect your name, email address, subject, and message content.
  • Newsletter Subscriptions: If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address.
  • Comments and Feedback: Any information you voluntarily provide when engaging with our content.

Information Collected Automatically:

  • Log Data: Your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages visited, time and date of visits, and time spent on pages.
  • Device Information: Device type, screen resolution, and unique device identifiers.
  • Cookies and Tracking Data: Information collected through cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies (see Section 3 below).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To Operate and Maintain Our Website: Ensuring our website functions properly and providing you with a seamless browsing experience.
  • To Respond to Inquiries: Addressing your messages submitted through our contact form.
  • To Improve Our Content: Analyzing website usage to understand what content resonates with our audience and how we can improve.
  • To Display Advertisements: Serving relevant advertisements through Google AdSense based on your interests and browsing behavior.
  • To Send Communications: If you opt in, sending newsletters and updates about new content on CricketHub.
  • To Comply with Legal Obligations: Fulfilling legal requirements and responding to lawful requests from authorities.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

CricketHub uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on our website. Cookies are small data files stored on your device that help us understand how you interact with our site.

Types of Cookies We Use:

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for the basic functionality of our website, such as remembering your cookie consent preference.
  • Analytics Cookies: Used by Google Analytics to track website traffic and user behavior (see Section 5).
  • Advertising Cookies: Used by Google AdSense and its partners to serve personalized advertisements based on your browsing history (see Section 4).
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences to improve your browsing experience.

Managing Cookies: You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies, delete cookies, or be notified when a cookie is set. Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

4. Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on CricketHub. Google AdSense is a third-party advertising service provided by Google LLC.

Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to CricketHub and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Additionally, you can opt out of Google's use of cookies by visiting Google's Ads Settings. For more information about how Google uses data when you visit partner sites, please review Google's Privacy & Terms.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website. Users may opt out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the Google ad and content network privacy policy.

5. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google LLC, to analyze website traffic and user behavior on CricketHub.

Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information such as how often users visit our site, what pages they visit, and what other sites they used prior to coming to ours. We use the information from Google Analytics to improve our website and content.

Google Analytics collects only the IP address assigned to you on the date you visit our site, rather than your name or other identifying information. Although Google Analytics plants a permanent cookie on your web browser to identify you as a unique user the next time you visit, the cookie cannot be used by anyone but Google.

Google's ability to use and share information collected by Google Analytics is restricted by the Google Analytics Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

6. Third-Party Links

CricketHub may contain links to third-party websites that are not operated by us. If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that third party's site. We strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of every site you visit.

We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services. These links are provided for your convenience and do not signify our endorsement of such websites or their content.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. We will retain and use your information to the extent necessary to:

  • Comply with our legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes
  • Enforce our policies
  • Fulfill the purposes for which it was collected

Contact form submissions are retained for a period of up to 12 months unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. Analytics data collected by Google Analytics is retained according to Google's data retention policies.

8. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information under applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights:

  • Right of Access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • Right to Rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate or complete information you believe is incomplete.
  • Right to Erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your personal data under certain conditions.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data under certain conditions.
  • Right to Data Portability: You have the right to request that we transfer the data we have collected to another organization, or directly to you, under certain conditions.
  • Right to Object: You have the right to object to our processing of your personal data under certain conditions.

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under CCPA:

  • Right to Know: You have the right to request that we disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you.
  • Right to Delete: You have the right to request the deletion of your personal information, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your CCPA rights.
  • Right to Opt-Out: You have the right to opt out of the sale of your personal information. CricketHub does not sell personal information.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at contact@crickethub.co.in. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

CricketHub does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13.

If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under the age of 13, we will take steps to delete such information as soon as possible. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at contact@crickethub.co.in so that we can take appropriate action.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page. Your continued use of CricketHub after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Information

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

  • Email: contact@crickethub.co.in
  • Website: crickethub.co.in
  • Contact Page: crickethub.co.in/pages/contact

We take your privacy seriously and will make every effort to address your concerns promptly and thoroughly.
