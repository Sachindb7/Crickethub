About CricketHub

Uncovering the untold stories of cricket — the emotions, the struggles, and the triumphs that mainstream media overlooks.

Our Mission

At CricketHub, our mission is simple yet powerful — to uncover the cricket stories that mainstream media ignores.

While the world focuses on scorecards and statistics, we dive deeper into the human side of cricket. The untold struggles behind a debut, the sacrifices families make for the sport, the locker-room moments that shaped legends — these are the stories that deserve to be told.

We believe every cricketer's journey is a story worth reading, from the gullies of Mumbai to the greens of Lord's. Our mission is to bring those stories to life — with depth, empathy, and authenticity.

Our Story

CricketHub started as a humble cricket satire blog — a place where fans could laugh, rant, and celebrate the beautiful absurdities of the sport we all love.

But as we grew, something changed. We realized that cricket fans didn't just want hot takes — they craved deep, meaningful storytelling. They wanted to know what happened behind the stumps, behind the cameras, and behind the headlines.

So we evolved. We traded clickbait for long-form narratives. We replaced gossip with research. And CricketHub transformed from a casual blog into a platform dedicated to the most compelling, untold stories in cricket history.

Today, CricketHub is where cricket fans come when they want more than just scores — they want the soul of the sport.

Meet the Team

Behind CricketHub is a small but passionate editorial team — a group of cricket analysts, researchers, and storytellers who live and breathe the sport.

We're not a faceless media corporation. We're cricket fans who happen to write. Every story we publish is crafted with the same passion you'd bring to a late-night cricket debate with your best mates.

  • Research Team — Deep-diving into cricket archives, autobiographies, and firsthand accounts
  • Writers & Storytellers — Turning research into gripping narratives that bring cricket's untold moments to life
  • Editorial Team — Ensuring every piece is factually accurate, compelling, and worthy of your time

Together, we form the CricketHub editorial team — united by one belief: cricket is more than a game, it's a collection of extraordinary human stories.

Our Values

Everything we do at CricketHub is guided by four core values:

  • Authenticity — We never fabricate or exaggerate. Every story is rooted in real events and verified facts. Our readers trust us because we earn it with every word.
  • Deep Research — We go beyond press conferences and Wikipedia pages. We dig into archives, read autobiographies, watch rare interviews, and connect the dots others miss.
  • Storytelling — Facts alone don't move people — stories do. We craft every piece to be an immersive, emotional experience that stays with you long after you've finished reading.
  • Cricket Love — At its core, CricketHub exists because we love cricket. That love infuses every article, every headline, and every late-night editing session.

Editorial Process

Quality is non-negotiable at CricketHub. Here's how every story goes from idea to publication:

  • Research & Discovery — Our team identifies compelling untold stories through cricket archives, historical records, autobiographies, interviews, and community tips.
  • Fact-Checking — Every claim, date, and quote is cross-referenced with multiple reliable sources. We verify before we publish, always.
  • Writing & Crafting — Our writers transform raw research into engaging, narrative-driven pieces that respect the subject and captivate the reader.
  • Editorial Review — Each article goes through a thorough editorial review for accuracy, readability, tone, and sensitivity before it goes live.
  • Reader Feedback — We actively listen to our readers. If something is inaccurate or can be improved, we update it transparently.

We take our responsibility seriously. When you read a CricketHub story, you can trust that it's been researched, reviewed, and published with care.
